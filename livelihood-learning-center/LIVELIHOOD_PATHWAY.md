# ForeRunners Livelihood & Career Pathway
## Product design decision — 5 November 2026

### Purpose
The platform is not intended to be a job-board-first website. It is a learner-centred livelihood development and career pathway system. It starts with the learner's current situation and provides staged, voluntary support towards decent work, viable self-employment or further learning.

### Agreed learner journey
1. **Assessment & profiling** — understand education, work history, existing and transferable skills, interests, goals, employment/income situation, digital access, barriers and support needs.
2. **Personalized guidance** — provide an explainable recommendation. Offer a human Career Counselor conversation when the learner requests it, the situation is complex, or the learner would benefit from human support. Refer to a Business Mentor or other specialist when appropriate. AI supports navigation; it does not make binding decisions.
3. **Learning pathway recommendation** — recommend business training, vocational training, foundational/digital skills or further learning based on assessed gaps and the learner's goals.
4. **People, Profit & Planet** — embed the triple-bottom-line approach in every relevant course:
   - People: dignity, inclusion, worker rights, safety, fair work and community benefit.
   - Profit: viable income, costing, cash flow, productivity, resilience and responsible growth.
   - Planet: resource efficiency, waste reduction, pollution prevention, climate/environmental responsibility.
   Training should include practical assignments demonstrating all three dimensions where relevant.
5. **Practical competency & portfolio** — assess demonstrated skills using transparent rubrics, work samples, practical tasks and trainer feedback. Course completion alone must not be treated as proof of job readiness.
6. **Opportunity matching** — match learners to internships, apprenticeships, professional positions, paid small projects, freelance opportunities and self-employment/enterprise support. Show requirements, compensation/paid status, time commitment, location/work mode, safeguarding expectations and deadlines clearly.
7. **Graduate follow-up & improvement** — check outcomes at 30, 90 and 180 days, with voluntary and confidential income reporting and separate consent for any public success story.

### Pathways
- Employment
- Self-employment / microenterprise
- Apprenticeship / work-based learning
- Further training / education
- Exploring / undecided

Learners may move between pathways. No pathway is assigned solely by an automated score.

### Safeguarding, fairness and privacy
- Explain why information is collected and who can see it.
- Collect only information needed for guidance and support.
- Keep portfolio and sensitive follow-up records private under existing access controls.
- Do not require learners to disclose income, disability, trauma or other sensitive details to receive ordinary learning support.
- Make human support available and allow learners to decline recommendations.
- Do not recommend unpaid work as if it were paid employment. Clearly label compensation and duration.
- Assess opportunity providers and projects for safe conditions, realistic tasks, fair treatment and environmental/social responsibility.
- Keep success-story publication consent separate from programme follow-up consent.

### Current implementation vs next development
Already present in the platform: learner skills profile, self-assessment, practical portfolio, trainer competency feedback, opportunity listings and applications, graduate follow-up surveys and aggregate outcome reporting.

The pathway overview is now visible in the learner workspace. The following are **next development items, not yet implemented as complete workflows**:
1. Structured multi-part intake assessment and saved assessment summary.
2. Counselor/mentor referral request, appointment scheduling and private case notes with role-based access.
3. Training catalogue tagged by pathway, skill level and People/Profit/Planet outcomes.
4. Recommendation workflow linking assessment gaps to training and support options, with human review.
5. Practical competency rubrics and readiness criteria linked to opportunity matching.
6. Opportunity categories for internships, professional roles, paid small projects, freelance and enterprise support, including transparent compensation and safeguarding fields.

### Implementation order
1. Improve assessment and learner profile.
2. Add human guidance request/referral workflow.
3. Build the People/Profit/Planet training catalogue and recommendations.
4. Strengthen practical assessment and portfolio.
5. Expand opportunity matching and application tracking.
6. Improve graduate follow-up and aggregate impact learning.

### Design principle
**Assess first. Guide together. Build skills responsibly. Demonstrate competence. Connect to decent opportunities. Follow up.**
