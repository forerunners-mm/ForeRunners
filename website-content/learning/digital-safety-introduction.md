# Digital Safety Basics
## Learning outcomes
Participants will be able to recognize common online risks, use strong passwords and multi-factor authentication (MFA), and identify suspicious links.
## Session plan
1. Opening discussion: What makes an account safe? (10 minutes)
2. Mini-lesson: Passwords, MFA and phishing (15 minutes)
3. Group exercise: Spot suspicious messages (15 minutes)
4. Practical activity: Review account security settings (15 minutes)
5. Reflection and closing (5 minutes)
## Knowledge check
1. What does MFA add to a password? (A) Another verification step (B) Faster internet (C) More followers. **Answer: A**
2. What should you do before opening an unexpected link? (A) Forward it (B) Verify the sender and destination (C) Share your password. **Answer: B**
3. True or false: Reusing one password for every account is recommended. **Answer: False**
## Facilitator note
Do not ask participants to share passwords or display private account details. Adapt examples to participants' local context.
