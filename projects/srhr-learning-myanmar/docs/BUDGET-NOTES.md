## 2. Required costing detail

For every budget line prepare activity, responsible partner, quantity, unit, unit cost, number of months/rounds, subtotal, currency, quotation/source, donor eligibility and justification. Staff costs need role, level of effort, monthly rate, months and allocation percentage. Shared costs need a transparent allocation method and must not be charged twice.

## 3. Quantities to confirm

- Participants: 600 total; planning assumption 100 per ward.
- Wards: six, names and access feasibility to be confirmed.
- Facilitated sessions: determine after curriculum and group-size decisions.
- Facilitators: determine safe group size, staffing ratio, training and travel needs.
- Community initiatives: define number, selection criteria, award size, controls and reporting.
- Materials: language, print/digital quantities and accessibility adaptations.
- Digital platform: cost the restricted MVP only; AI is excluded from initial child-facing phase.
- Baseline/endline: sample size, tools, enumerator days, analysis and dissemination.
- Safeguarding: focal person time, training, referral support, accessible complaints and incident response.
- Travel: actual routes, trips, staff and safety arrangements.

## 4. Budget safeguards

1. Confirm donor limits for overhead, contingency, equipment, staff, subgrants, exchange rates and taxes.
2. Obtain competitive quotations where required.
3. Ensure every cost is necessary, reasonable, allocable and documented.
4. Do not pay for AI functionality outside approved and safely deliverable scope.
5. Confirm the lead applicant can legally receive and manage funds.
6. Document procurement, conflict-of-interest and approval procedures.
7. Reconcile partner budgets with the workplan and signed agreement.
8. Keep contingency separate and use only with written approval if donor rules allow.
9. Retain invoices, contracts, output evidence and approvals under the approved records schedule.

## 5. Cost-effectiveness

USD 120,000 divided by 600 proposed participants equals USD 200 per participant. This arithmetic is not a unit-cost justification: the project also includes six-ward engagement, safeguarding, referral readiness, platform development and monitoring. A value-for-money explanation must use the final activity-based budget and verified reach.
