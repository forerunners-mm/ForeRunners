# Project Concept Note: SRHR Learning Myanmar

## Overview

- **Lead:** Nway Htway Eain Organization (NHE)
- **Technical support:** ForeRunners Organization (FR)
- **Duration:** 12 months
- **Location:** Six wards in Insein Township, Yangon Region, Myanmar
- **Primary target:** 600 adolescent girls
- **Proposed budget:** USD 120,000
- **Focus:** Sexual and reproductive health and rights (SRHR), adolescent learning and women's empowerment

## Goal

Improve adolescent girls' access to accurate, age-appropriate SRHR information and strengthen knowledge, confidence and informed decision-making through community-based engagement and digital learning.

## Objectives

1. Reach 600 adolescent girls with accessible SRHR information.
2. Deliver community awareness sessions and locally led initiatives.
3. Pilot a digital SRHR Learning Center with lessons, quizzes and educational games.
4. Provide a carefully bounded AI-assisted learning companion grounded in reviewed educational materials.
5. Measure learning outcomes and strengthen safe referral pathways.

## Responsibilities

### NHE — Lead organization
- Community entry, outreach and participant engagement.
- Community awareness sessions.
- Community initiative activities.
- Coordination with caregivers and community stakeholders where safe and appropriate.
- Local monitoring, safeguarding and referral coordination.
- Overall project leadership and reporting under the grant agreement.

### FR — Technical support partner
- Website and digital learning platform.
- Learning, quiz and game modules.
- AI assistant design, only after content approval and safety testing.
- Digital progress and aggregate reporting tools.
- Technical training, maintenance and troubleshooting.

## Expected outcomes

- 600 adolescent girls reached, subject to validated participation records.
- Community awareness and initiative activities delivered across six wards.
- A reviewed and tested digital SRHR learning prototype.
- Improved SRHR knowledge measured through baseline and endline assessment.
- Appropriate referral information and safeguarding procedures available.

These are proposed targets, not achieved results.

## Safeguarding and privacy

The project will use age-appropriate materials, data minimization, access controls, human-reviewed SRHR content, confidential referral processes and clear procedures for protection disclosures. Participation should not require a personal smartphone. The platform must not solicit intimate images or publish identifiable learner data. AI responses must not diagnose, prescribe or replace professional care.
